The problem

Exchanging pesos or dollars for digital assets with a stranger.

One person posts an ad and another one takes it.

What I did

The interfaces, the documentation and the visual QA.

The result

A blockchain platform that feels easy for thousands of users.

From 500 to more than 7,000 users between May 2023 and April 2025.

Tools: Figma, Notion and Slack.

In 2023, exchanging money with a stranger on blockchain took a lot of trust. I built it with clear states, one action per step, the chat inside the trade, and communities. Users praised every improvement and pointed out how easy everything was to understand.
